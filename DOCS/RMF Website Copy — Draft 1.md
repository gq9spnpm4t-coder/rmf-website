## 4. About me

**Heading:** The person behind the lens

**Copy:**
I started Ridenour Media Firm at sixteen, the summer after I got pulled onto a friend's film set. I came for the acting and stayed for everything happening behind the camera — I was hooked on the technical craft of it. So I taught myself: bought the camera, the lights, the mics, the gimbal, and spent every hour I could learning how to actually use them.

The first real job I ever did was the Tulsa Maker's Faire. I reached out, offered to cover it, and showed up ready to work — and that one event turned into a relationship, an internship, and a lot more work after it. That's still how I like to operate: show up, care about the project, and let the work earn the next one.

I run the whole process myself — planning, shooting, editing, delivery — so you're always talking to the person actually doing the work.
