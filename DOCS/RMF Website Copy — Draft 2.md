## 4. About me

**Heading:** The person behind the lens

**Copy:**
I started Ridenour Media Firm at sixteen, the summer after I got pulled onto a friend's film set. I came for the acting and stayed for everything happening behind the camera. I was hooked on the technical craft of it, so I taught myself. I bought the camera, the lights, the mics, the gimbal, and spent every hour I could learning how to actually use them.

The first real job I ever did was the Tulsa Maker's Faire. I reached out, offered to cover it, and showed up ready to work. That one event turned into a real relationship and a lot more work after it.

The way I like to operate is simple. I want to serve the project, but more than that, I want to understand what you're actually trying to do. When I really get what you're going for, I can help in ways that benefit the whole thing, not just hand you a video or a photo. I run the entire process myself, from planning to shooting to editing to delivery, so you're always talking to the person doing the work.
