# Ridenour Media Firm — Website Content Interview (Voice)

**How to use this:** Paste everything below into a regular Claude chat and start a voice conversation. Let Claude interview you like a real conversation. At the end it will produce a written summary — copy that summary back into the Cowork session so the copy and Claude Code handoff can be written.

---

## Context for Claude (read this, then run the interview)

You are helping me gather content for a full redesign of my business website. Interview me conversationally by **voice** — warm, natural, one question at a time, with genuine follow-ups. Do NOT dump all questions at once. Ask, listen, dig deeper where it's interesting, and move on. Keep it feeling like a real interview, not a form.

**The business:**
- Ridenour Media Firm (RMF) — a Tulsa, Oklahoma based video + photo production studio.

- Heavy on **sports videography** (shot 1080p120 on a Sony a7 III).
- Clients include government/organizations (e.g. Tulsa County) plus local businesses and creators.
- Socials: YouTube @JunuhRidenour, Instagram @ridenour_media_firm.

- Tone/voice for all copy: **warm & personal** — leads with me as the filmmaker, relationship-driven, approachable for local clients.

**Your job in this interview:** get rich, quotable, specific material in my own voice. Chase stories and concrete details. Avoid generic marketing-speak.

## Interview guide (adapt naturally; follow the conversation)

1. **My story.** Why I started RMF. My background as a filmmaker and what pulled me toward video. What I want a client to know about me as a person.
2. **What makes working with me different** — in my own words. (Speed? Honest pricing? One person handling everything start to finish? Being local? A specific visual style?) Push me for concrete examples, not adjectives.
3. **Ideal client.** Who I most want more of (athletes/teams, local businesses, real estate agents, government/orgs, creators). Which services are my bread-and-butter to feature vs. ones I offer but don't need to lead with.
4. **Proof.** Clients I can name (Tulsa County?), projects I'm proud of, and specific portfolio pieces that should anchor the site. Get me to describe the work and why it mattered.
5. **Pricing.** Whether to show packages / "starting at" prices, or keep it inquiry-only for now.
6. **Intake form.** Keep the "start building your vision" flow? What it should ask, and where leads should land (which email/phone).
7. **Process & turnaround.** Is "tight workflow, you know exactly what you're getting and when" still the promise? Any typical turnaround I'm comfortable stating.
8. **Anything else** about the brand, the vibe, competitors I like/dislike, or how I want people to *feel* on the site.

Ask follow-ups whenever an answer is vague or interesting. Aim for depth over speed.
